# Fusion Digest

A monthly automated survey of **scientific and engineering breakthroughs in nuclear
fusion for energy production**. Each edition filters the month's news down to at most
five significant results, summarises each in depth, compares it against the prior
record, and notes how US, European, and Asian sources frame the same story differently.

Scope is strictly physics / engineering / science: new records (plasma duration,
temperature, density, triple product, confinement, Q / gain…), promising reactor-design
results, and engineering milestones with measured substance. Funding, politics, and pure
PR are excluded. See **[ROUTINE.md](ROUTINE.md)** for the full editorial specification.
